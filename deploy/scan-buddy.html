What is Scan Buddy?

Scan Buddy is a smart QR code for your business. Customers scan it once to follow your Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Xiaohongshu, message you on WhatsApp or leave a Google review, and you see how many people scanned.

Do my customers need an app?

No. Any phone camera can scan the QR code and open your page in the browser.

Yes. Update your links any time and the same printed QR code keeps working.
