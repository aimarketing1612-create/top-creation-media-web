FAQ

Questions, answered.

Should we outsource social media or train our own team?

Outsource if you need results now and have no one to run it. Train your team if you want to own the skill and cut agency costs long term. Many companies do both: we run it first, then hand it over.

Can you manage social media for a franchise or multiple outlets?

Yes. One brand strategy and content library for the group, with local ads, pages and reports for each outlet, so every branch looks the same and you can compare results.

Which social media platform should a Malaysian business start with?

Start where your buyers already are. Facebook and Instagram for precise targeting, TikTok for reach and live selling, Google for people already searching, Xiaohongshu for Chinese-speaking buyers.

Can we start with Done-For-You and move in-house later?

Yes. We run your accounts, then train your staff on the same accounts and templates, so the handover is smooth and nothing is lost.

How do you report social media results?

A clear monthly report with leads, sales, cost per result and return on ad spend, not just likes and views. You see every account and every number.
