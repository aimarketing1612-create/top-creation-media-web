FAQ

Questions, answered.

Should I choose Done-For-You or the Marketing Academy?

Choose Done-For-You if you want results without spending your own time on the tools. Choose the Academy if you want you or your staff to own the skill and save on agency fees long term. If you are unsure, WhatsApp us your goal and we will recommend one honestly.

Can I do both?

Yes. Many clients start with Done-For-You to get results quickly, then train their staff in the Academy so they can take over day-to-day work.

Which platform should I start with?

It depends on where your customers spend time and how they buy. Google suits people already searching, Facebook and Instagram suit precise targeting, TikTok suits reach and live selling, and Xiaohongshu suits Chinese-speaking buyers who search for recommendations.
