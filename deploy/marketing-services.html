FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does digital marketing cost in Malaysia?

It depends on your goals, the platforms you need, and how much content is involved. A business running Facebook and Instagram ads has very different needs from a brand that wants TikTok livestreams, SEO and video production together. That's why we build a customized package for every client. WhatsApp us with your goals and budget range, and we'll recommend the most cost-effective mix.

What is the minimum ad budget for Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok ads in Malaysia?

You can start small, but your budget needs to be big enough for the platform's algorithm to learn. We usually recommend a test budget first, finding the winning creatives and audiences, then scaling what works. On a strategy call we'll suggest a realistic starting budget for your industry and goals.

Which is better for my business: Facebook ads, TikTok ads, or Google ads?

It depends on how your customers buy. Google Ads captures people already searching for your product, which is great for high-intent leads. Facebook and Instagram ads are excellent for precise targeting and retargeting. TikTok is ideal for viral reach, younger audiences and live selling. Most brands get the best results from a mix, and we'll recommend the right split.

How do you track and report campaign results?

We measure what matters to your business: leads, sales, cost per result and return on ad spend (ROAS), not just likes and views. Using Meta Pixel, Google Analytics and platform dashboards, we track every channel and share clear reports, so you always know where your money goes and what it brings back.
