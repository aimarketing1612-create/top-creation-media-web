Questions, answered.

Do I need any technical or coding experience?

No. The classes start from where you are. If you can use WhatsApp and a browser, you can follow along. The website and automation modules use AI to do the technical parts, and we explain each step in plain language.

Which AI tools do you teach: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini?

All three, plus the video, design, website and automation tools that sit around them. We teach the method first, because tools change every month. The tool list is refreshed at each intake so you learn what works now.

Can you train my whole team or company?

Yes. We can run the classes for a team and tailor the examples to your industry, your brand and your data rules. Tell us your team size and goals on WhatsApp and we will suggest the right modules.

Which language are the classes in?

Top Creation Media works in English, 中文 and Bahasa Malaysia. Tell us your preference when you enquire and we will confirm what is available for your class.

How much do the classes cost and when is the next one?

Dates and fees depend on the modules you choose and your group size. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send the current schedule and a quote.

Will AI replace my marketing team?

AI replaces repetitive tasks, not judgment. The people who learn to direct AI well produce more, faster and at a higher standard. That is the skill these classes build.
