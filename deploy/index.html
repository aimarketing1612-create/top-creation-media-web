FAQ

Questions, answered.

Why choose us over other digital marketing agencies in Malaysia and Singapore?

With years of proven industry experience, we don't just run ads; we build full-funnel marketing ecosystems. From high-converting Facebook and Instagram strategies to Google SEO, we combine creative video production with hard data to deliver measurable results for clients across Malaysia, Singapore and beyond.

What is the difference between your marketing services and your marketing academy?

With our Done-For-You services, our team runs your marketing for you: strategy, content, ads, livestream and KOC/KOL. With the Marketing Academy, we teach you or your staff to do it yourselves, step by step, on your own accounts. Many clients start with one and add the other later.

How much does digital marketing cost in Malaysia?

It depends on your goals, the platforms you need, and how much content is involved. A business running Facebook and Instagram ads has very different needs from a brand that wants TikTok livestreams, SEO and video production together. That's why we build a customized package for every client. WhatsApp us with your goals and budget range, and we'll recommend the most cost-effective mix.

What is the minimum ad budget for Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok ads in Malaysia?

You can start small, but your budget needs to be big enough for the platform's algorithm to learn. We usually recommend a test budget first, finding the winning creatives and audiences, then scaling what works. On a strategy call we'll suggest a realistic starting budget for your industry and goals.

Which is better for my business: Facebook ads, TikTok ads, or Google ads?

It depends on how your customers buy. Google Ads captures people already searching for your product, which is great for high-intent leads. Facebook and Instagram ads are excellent for precise targeting and retargeting. TikTok is ideal for viral reach, younger audiences and live selling. Most brands get the best results from a mix, and we'll recommend the right split.

Do I need any experience or coding to join your AI or marketing classes?

No. The classes start from zero. If you can use WhatsApp and a browser, you can follow along. You practise on your own business with us guiding every step, and if you follow the classes and still can't do it yourself, we refund your class fee in full (terms apply).

Do you provide TikTok Live streaming and TikTok Shop services in Malaysia?

Yes. We run the full TikTok flywheel, from trend-driven short videos to complete livestream operations in our in-house livestream studio. We handle live hosts, scripting, TikTok Shop catalog setup, affiliate creators and paid traffic to your live sessions, turning viewers into real sales.

Can you help my brand market on Xiaohongshu (RED) to reach Chinese-speaking customers?

Yes. Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book) is one of the most powerful platforms for reaching Chinese-speaking consumers in Malaysia, Singapore and China. We create authentic, native-style notes, work with KOCs and KOLs, and plan search-led content so customers who look for recommendations discover your brand.

Do you work with KOLs and influencers (KOC & KOL marketing)?

Yes. We match your brand with the right KOLs (key opinion leaders) and KOCs (key opinion consumers) based on audience fit, engagement quality and budget, not just follower count. We manage briefing, content approval and result tracking, so every collaboration drives real awareness and sales.

Does your team handle video production and event marketing?

Yes. Our in-house team handles video production, from short-form reels to brand films. We also run event marketing: capturing offline events and amplifying them online through targeted search and social campaigns, taking your brand from offline to online.

Do you offer Google SEO and SEM, including AI search?

Yes. We cover SEO (search engine optimization), SEM (Google Ads), AEO (answer engine optimization) and GEO (generative engine optimization), so your brand shows up both in Google results and in AI answers from ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI.

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

SEO is a long-term investment. Most websites start to see movement in Google rankings and organic traffic within 3 to 6 months, depending on competition and the current state of the site. Paid ads on Google, Meta and TikTok can bring traffic within days, which is why we often combine SEM for fast leads with SEO for sustainable growth.

What are Scan Buddy and AI Video?

They are AI tools for TCM subscribers. Scan Buddy is a smart QR code that sends customers to all your social media, WhatsApp and Google reviews in one scan, and shows you every scan. AI Video turns a product photo into ready-to-post marketing videos. WhatsApp us for access.

How do you track and report campaign results?

We measure what matters to your business: leads, sales, cost per result and return on ad spend (ROAS), not just likes and views. Using Meta Pixel, Google Analytics and platform dashboards, we track every channel and share clear reports, so you always know where your money goes and what it brings back.

Can you help local brands expand into international markets?

Yes. We combine Google SEO, localized search ads and targeted Instagram, Facebook and TikTok campaigns to introduce your brand to new markets, with clients across Malaysia, Singapore, China, the USA and Indonesia.
