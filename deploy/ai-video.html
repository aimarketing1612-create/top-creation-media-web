Questions, answered.

Do I need to film anything?

No. Start from a product photo. AI creates the scenes, motion, captions and music. You can also add your own footage if you have it.

What kind of videos can I make?

Product showcases, new backgrounds and scenes, AI model and outfit try-ons, UGC-style clips and short ads with captions, in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9.

What photos work best?

Clear, well-lit product photos on a plain background give the best results. Our team can help review and polish videos when needed.
