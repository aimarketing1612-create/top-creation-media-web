Kling AI and Veo 3: how to turn a product photo into a video ad

You no longer need a film crew for a product video that stops the scroll. This is the workflow we teach for turning one product photo into short ads with Kling AI and Google's Veo 3.

Updated 25 Nov 20263 min readBy the Top Creation Media team
Short answer

Kling AI (from Kuaishou) and Veo 3 (from Google, available through Gemini and Google's video tools) turn text and images into short video clips. Kling is strong at animating your own product photos; Veo 3 can also generate sound and spoken lines. Use them for product reveals, B-roll and ad variations, not to fake reviews.

Kling AI vs Veo 3 at a glance

Both tools update often, and plans, credits and limits change. Check the official sites before you budget for a campaign.

The 6-step workflow

  1. Start with a clean product photo. Good light, sharp focus, a plain background and the product filling most of the frame. AI can only animate what it can see.
  2. Write a shot, not a wish. Describe the camera move, what the product does, the setting, the light and the length. "Make it nice" gives you random results.
  3. Generate three to five short clips per idea. Five to eight seconds each. Short clips fail less, and you will cut them anyway.
  4. Pick the best and check every frame. Look at the label, the shape and the colour. If the product looks different from the real one, do not use it in an ad.
  5. Edit it into an ad. In CapCut or Canva: a hook in the first two seconds, captions, your logo, the offer and a clear call to action, in vertical 9:16.
  6. Test two or three versions. Run them as ads with the same budget for a few days and keep the one with the best cost per enquiry.

A prompt template you can copy

Prompt

Slow push-in on [product] standing on [surface], [lighting, e.g. soft morning window light], [one movement, e.g. water droplets running down the bottle], shallow depth of field, premium commercial look, vertical 9:16, 6 seconds, no text.

Video models usually follow English prompts most closely, so keep the prompt in English even when the ad itself is in Malay or Chinese. Add the captions and voice-over in your language when you edit.

What AI video does well, and where it fails

  • Good at: product reveals, mood shots, new backgrounds and seasons, and quick variations for ad testing.
  • Still weak at: readable text and logos, hands holding small items, and keeping the exact product details from shot to shot.
  • Never use it to: invent customer testimonials, show results the product cannot deliver, or copy another brand's look.
  • Label it where required. Meta and TikTok ask advertisers to label realistic AI-generated content. Follow each platform's current rules.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Kling AI free?

Kling has offered free credits with limits on length, quality and speed, and paid plans for more and better output. Plans change often, so check the official site for current pricing.

Can I use AI video in Facebook and TikTok ads?

Yes, as long as the video does not mislead. Show the real product accurately, do not fake testimonials, and label realistic AI content where the platform asks you to.

Do I need video editing skills?

Basic skills are enough. Generating clips takes prompts, and turning them into an ad takes a free editor such as CapCut or Canva. Our AI classes cover both.
