What is AEO and GEO? How to get your business recommended by AI

Customers no longer only type keywords into Google. They ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews "which agency should I hire?" or "best facial in Puchong?". AEO and GEO are how you become the answer.

Updated 25 Nov 20263 min readBy the Top Creation Media team
Short answer

AEO (answer engine optimisation) means shaping your content so AI assistants and Google's answer features can quote it. GEO (generative engine optimisation) means making your brand show up, and be recommended, inside AI-written answers. Both build on good SEO; they do not replace it.

Why it matters now in Malaysia and Singapore

  • AI answers name only a few businesses. A Google page shows ten results; an AI answer usually recommends three to five. If you are not one of them, the customer may never see you.
  • "Best of" lists carry weight. When we researched "best digital marketing agency Malaysia", most of page one was list articles and directories, and AI assistants lean on the same sources.
  • Customers ask in three languages. People ask in English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese. Content in only one language leaves the other answers to your competitors.

7 steps to get recommended by AI

  1. Answer real questions plainly. Use the customer's question as a heading and answer it in the first two sentences, then add detail.
  2. Add structured data. Organization, FAQ, product and local business markup tell search engines and AI exactly who you are and what you sell.
  3. Keep your facts the same everywhere. Name, phone, address, opening hours and prices should match on your website, Google Business Profile, Facebook and every directory.
  4. Get mentioned on sites AI trusts. Industry directories, "best of" articles, local news and partner websites are where AI learns which businesses are credible.
  5. Earn reviews that say something. Ask happy customers to mention the service and the result, not just give five stars.
  6. Let AI crawlers in. Make sure robots.txt does not block GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot or Google, and add an llms.txt summary of your business.
  7. Publish in your customers' languages. A proper Chinese or Malay version of your key pages is one of the fastest ways to show up in more answers.

How to check whether AI mentions you

Once a month, ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your customers would ask, in each language you serve. Note who gets named and which websites are cited. Those cited sites are your to-do list for step four. Keep watching Google Search Console too: AI features still draw on the same index.

Common mistakes

  • Stuffing keywords. AI rewards clear answers, not repeated phrases.
  • Blocking AI bots by accident. Some security plugins and old robots.txt files do this without you knowing.
  • Different prices in different places. Conflicting facts make AI less sure about recommending you.

AEO and GEO are a long game: expect months, not days. We applied every step above to our own website when we rebuilt it, in English and Chinese.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is AEO replacing SEO?

No. AI assistants and Google's AI Overviews draw on the same web pages that SEO improves. AEO and GEO add clear answers, structured data and brand mentions on top of good SEO.

How long does AEO or GEO take to work?

Usually a few months. Technical fixes such as structured data and robots.txt work quickly, while mentions on trusted sites and reviews build up over time.

Can a small business show up in ChatGPT answers?

Yes, especially for local and niche questions such as "best bridal makeup in Johor Bahru". Clear answers on your site, a complete Google Business Profile and genuine reviews matter more than company size.
