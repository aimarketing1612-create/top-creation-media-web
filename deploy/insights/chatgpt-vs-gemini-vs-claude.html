ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude: which AI should your business use?

All three can write, summarise, analyse and brainstorm. The real difference is where each one fits into your working day. This is how we help business owners choose in our AI classes.

Updated 25 Nov 20263 min readBy the Top Creation Media team
Short answer

If your team already lives in Gmail, Docs and Sheets, start with Gemini. If you write long proposals or work with big documents, try Claude. If you want one all-rounder with images, voice and a huge library of add-ons, start with ChatGPT. Most businesses end up using two of them.

At a glance

ChatGPTGeminiClaude
Best atAll-round writing, images, voice and custom GPTsWorking inside Google Search, Gmail, Docs, Sheets and DriveLong documents, careful writing and detailed analysis
Good first taskWrite 10 versions of an ad captionSummarise last month's enquiry emails into a sheetTurn a 40-page proposal into a one-page brief
Watch out forConfident answers that are wrong: check facts and numbersQuality differs from one Google app to anotherFewer built-in image and media tools

Plans and features change almost every month, so treat this as a starting point and check each official page for current prices in ringgit or Singapore dollars.

How to choose in 15 minutes

  1. Pick three real tasks. Use work you did this week: a promotion caption, a reply to a customer complaint, a summary of a supplier quote.
  2. Give all three the same prompt. Copy the exact same instructions and files into each one, so you compare the AI and not your wording.
  3. Score the answers. Rate each answer for accuracy, tone and how much editing it still needs. Time saved is the number that matters.
  4. Check your data rules. Before the team uses it every day, decide what may and may not be pasted in, and whether you need a business plan with data controls.

Using AI safely with business data

  • Keep personal data out. Do not paste IC numbers, bank details or full customer lists into a personal account.
  • Use team or business plans for company work. They give you admin controls and clearer terms on how your data is used.
  • A human checks before anything goes out. AI drafts, people approve: prices, promises and claims must be right.
  • Save the prompts that work. A shared prompt library turns one good result into a team habit.

Three prompts to try today

Prompt

You are a marketing copywriter for a [type of business] in Kuala Lumpur. Write 10 short Facebook ad captions for [product or offer]. Mix English and simple Malay where it sounds natural. Each caption under 125 characters, with one clear call to action.

Prompt

Here are 20 customer reviews. Group them into the top 5 things customers love and the top 3 complaints. For each complaint, suggest one fix we can make this month.

Prompt

Turn this product description into a 30-second video script: a hook in the first 3 seconds, three benefits, and a closing line with our WhatsApp number.

What about English, Malay and Chinese?

All three handle English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese well enough for everyday business writing. Tone is where they differ, so test a caption in the language your customers actually use, and ask a native speaker on your team to read it before it goes live.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which AI is best for a small business in Malaysia?

There is no single winner. Gemini suits teams that work in Google Workspace, Claude suits long documents and careful writing, and ChatGPT is the most complete all-rounder. Run the same three real tasks in each and keep the one that saves your team the most time.

Is the free version enough?

For trying things out, yes. For daily company work, a paid business or team plan is usually worth it: higher limits, better models and admin controls over company data.

Can these AI tools write in Bahasa Melayu and Chinese?

Yes. All three write in English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese. Check the tone with a native speaker before publishing, especially for ads and customer replies.
