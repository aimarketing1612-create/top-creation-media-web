Questions, answered.

Is this for complete beginners?

Yes. Each platform starts from account setup and builds step by step to optimising and scaling. If you already run ads, you can jump straight to the module you need.

Do I need an ad budget to join?

You learn the setup and structure without spending, but real learning comes from a small live test. We will help you choose a sensible test budget for your industry and goal.

Which platforms do you cover?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Xiaohongshu (RED), from account setup to ads, tracking and reporting. For Google SEO and AI-powered search, see our AI classes.

Can I send my staff or run it for my company?

Yes. We can run the classes for your team and use your own products and accounts as the practice material. Message us your team size and goals.

How much do the classes cost and when do they start?

Dates and fees depend on the platforms you choose and group size. Message us on WhatsApp for the current schedule and a quote.

Tell us your business and goal. We will point you to the right platform and starting budget.
