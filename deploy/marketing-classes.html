Facebook & Instagram, from account to scale

Nine steps from a blank account to campaigns you can optimise and grow. Facebook and Instagram share the same Meta Ads Manager.

01

Business setup

A clean, verified account structure.

What you learn

Set up your Meta Business Portfolio, Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and ad account the right way: payment, roles and domain verification, so nothing gets restricted later.

02

Tracking

A tested pixel and server events.

What you learn

Install the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, choose the right events (Lead, Purchase, Message) and check them in Events Manager before you spend a ringgit.

03

Campaign structure

A campaign built from a proven structure.

What you learn

Pick the right objective, understand Advantage+ versus manual campaigns, and set up budgets, ad sets and ads with names you can still read in six months.

04

Audiences

Three audiences, tested against each other.

What you learn

Broad versus interest targeting, custom audiences, lookalikes, exclusions, and location and language settings for Malaysia and Singapore.

05

Creative that converts

A creative testing plan and your first ads.

What you learn

Hooks, formats and copy for video, Reels, Stories, carousel and static ads. How to run a creative test so every ad teaches you something.

06

Launch

A campaign live, with a launch checklist.

What you learn

Placements, schedule and budget, click-to-WhatsApp and Messenger destinations, ad policy pitfalls and a pre-launch checklist.

07

Instagram growth & DMs

A Reels plan and a DM flow that answers in seconds.

What you learn

Reels that get watched, partnership ads with creators, product tags and DM auto-replies that turn interest into sales.

08

Reading the numbers

A weekly report you can read in five minutes.

What you learn

ROAS, cost per lead, CTR, CPM and frequency: what each tells you, which to ignore, and how attribution changes what you see.

09

Optimise & scale

A 30-day optimisation plan.

What you learn

When to switch off, when to wait and when to scale. Creative refresh, budget steps, retargeting and A/B tests.

TikTok content, ads and live selling

From Business Center to Smart+ and GMV Max, with videos that feel native and a plan for TikTok Live.

01

Business setup

A working Business Center and ad account.

What you learn

Create your TikTok Business Center and Ads Manager account and link your profile. Connect TikTok Shop if you sell products.

02

Tracking

Verified conversion tracking.

What you learn

Install the TikTok Pixel and Events API, pick your conversion events and test them before launch.

03

Content that feels native

Five native-style videos.

What you learn

Hook-first video that looks like TikTok, not like an ad: scripting, filming on a phone, editing and trends.

04

Campaign types

A campaign plan for your business.

What you learn

Manual campaigns versus Smart+, Spark Ads, GMV Max for TikTok Shop and LIVE ads. Which to use at which stage.

05

TikTok Live & Shop

A livestream run-of-show.

What you learn

Plan a selling live: run-of-show, host script, product lineup, offers and how to drive traffic to your LIVE.

06

KOC collaborations

A KOC brief and shortlist.

What you learn

Find, brief and work with KOCs and affiliates, then turn their best posts into Spark Ads.

07

Results & refresh

A weekly report and refresh schedule.

What you learn

Read cost per result and ROAS, spot creative fatigue early and refresh before performance drops.

Google SEO, search ads and AI search

Get found when customers search, with ChatGPT doing the heavy lifting on research and writing.

01

Google basics & Business Profile

A verified, complete Business Profile.

What you learn

How Google search works, and how to set up your Google Business Profile so you appear on Maps with reviews and photos.

02

Keyword research with ChatGPT

A keyword map for your website.

What you learn

Find what your customers actually search, then use ChatGPT or Claude with Keyword Planner to group keywords and plan your pages.

03

SEO on your website

An optimised page, live.

What you learn

Titles, descriptions, headings, content, page speed and internal links, checked in Search Console.

04

AI search: AEO & GEO

A search-ready FAQ page.

What you learn

FAQs, structured data and clear answers so you appear in Google AI answers, ChatGPT and Gemini.

05

Google Ads: search

A search campaign ready to launch.

What you learn

Search campaigns, keywords and match types, ad copy, assets and conversion tracking.

06

Performance Max & YouTube

A plan for your next campaign.

What you learn

When Performance Max and YouTube ads make sense, and how to feed them good creative.

07

Measure & improve

A monthly review template.

What you learn

GA4, Search Console and cost per lead: read your sample website results and decide what to fix next.

Xiaohongshu (RED): notes, 博主 and ads

Reach Chinese-speaking customers with notes that get found, creators who build trust, and paid support.

01

Account & positioning

A positioned, ready-to-post profile.

What you learn

Personal versus professional accounts, a profile that says who you help, and what the platform expects from brands.

02

Search-led notes

Five notes built around real search keywords.

What you learn

Xiaohongshu is a search platform. Keyword research, note titles and covers, tags, image versus video notes, and a posting rhythm.

03

博主 & KOC seeding

A creator brief and shortlist.

What you learn

Seed genuine content through creators before spending on ads: briefs, selection, approvals and the Pugongying (蒲公英) platform.

04

Juguang (聚光) ads

A paid plan and access route.

What you learn

Search versus feed ads, keyword bidding and targeting, and how overseas brands access Juguang through certified partners.

05

Compliance & disclosure

A compliance checklist.

What you learn

Ad and sponsored-content rules, claims you cannot make, and how to stay within platform policy.

06

Leads & measurement

A lead path and monthly review.

What you learn

Turn attention into enquiries with DMs, lead forms and Qianfan (千帆), then track note performance and cost per lead.
